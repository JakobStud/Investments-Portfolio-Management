# Learning Roadmap

This roadmap defines the learning sequence for the lab. I will implement every concept and all related code myself.

1. Returns, expected return, variance, standard deviation, Sharpe ratio
2. Covariance, correlation, and diversification
3. Portfolio risk and risk contribution
4. Investment opportunity set
5. Efficient frontier
6. Risk-free asset and optimal risky portfolio
7. Single Index Model and beta
8. Systematic vs. idiosyncratic risk
9. Risk pooling, risk sharing, and time diversification
10. CAPM
11. Arbitrage Pricing Theory and factor models
12. Fama-French 3- and 5-factor models

Notebook placeholders are provided in `notebooks/` for each stage.
